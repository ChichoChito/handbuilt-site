Questions homeowners actually ask

How do I know I'm not missing something important?

Every decision, materials, layout, fixtures, gets finalized on paper before construction begins. If it's not on the plan, it doesn't get built without your sign-off.

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Los Angeles?

Every kitchen we build is custom to your home and your life, so is the cost. Rather than give you a number that might not apply to your project, we provide a detailed written estimate after an in-home visit, specific to what you're actually building.

Do you handle the design too, or do I need to hire an architect separately?

Yes. Design and construction happen under one agreement, with one team responsible from first sketch to final walk-through. You're not coordinating between a separate architect and contractor.

Can I stay in my home during the remodel?

Most kitchens allow you to stay in your home, though expect a temporary kitchen setup during the most disruptive phases. We walk through what that looks like for your specific layout during the Discovery Call.

What happens if I want to change something after construction starts?

Changes are always possible. They go through a written change order with pricing agreed before the work happens, so you never see a surprise charge for something you didn't approve.

How do payments work?

Payment is tied to project milestones, not a single upfront lump sum. You know what you're paying for and when, at every stage.

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

Most kitchen remodels take 3 to 5 months from permit to completion. You get a real timeline before construction starts, not one that shifts once you're already committed.

What if I already got other quotes?

We're happy to walk through them with you, so you know exactly what you're comparing.
